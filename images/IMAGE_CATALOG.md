## Part A: Per-Experiment Figures

### A1_exp2_overfitting.png
**Title:** Exp 2: More Epochs Exposed Severe Overfitting
**Contents:** Single-axis line chart showing Exp 2 train loss (pink) vs val loss (blue) over 35 epochs. A shaded pink region between the curves highlights the growing train-val divergence. An annotation box notes the severe overfitting (train loss ~0.63, val loss ~2.38 by epoch 35, a 3.8x gap).
**Used in:** Section 4.2 (Experiment 2)

### A2_exp2v3_overfitting_fix.png
**Title:** The Biggest Improvement: Training Strategy Overhaul (+20.6% mIoU)
**Contents:** Side-by-side dual-panel figure. Left panel: Exp 2 train vs val loss (diverging, 3.8x gap). Right panel: Exp 3 train vs val loss (parallel, near 1:1 ratio). Both panels have shaded gap regions and annotation boxes summarising the train-val ratio. Demonstrates the impact of 500x weight decay increase (1e-4 to 0.05).
**Used in:** Section 4.3 (Experiment 3)

### A3_exp3v4_perclass.png
**Title:** Exp 3->4: ResNet50 Helped Minor Damage, But Major/Destroyed Regressed
**Contents:** Grouped bar chart comparing per-class F1 scores (No Damage, Minor Damage, Major Damage, Destroyed) between Exp 3 (ResNet34, blue) and Exp 4 (ResNet50, green). Annotated with "+53%" on Minor Damage improvement (0.28 to 0.43) and "-17%" on Major Damage regression (0.46 to 0.38).
**Used in:** Section 4.4 (Experiment 4)

### A4_exp4v5_phase2_adaptation.png
**Title:** Exp 4 vs 5: More Aggressive Fine-Tuning Unlocks ResNet50
**Contents:** Line chart of val damage macro F1 during Phase 2 epochs for Exp 4 (green, encoder_lr=1e-5, 2 layers) and Exp 5 (pink, encoder_lr=5e-5, 4 layers + Focal). Star markers at best epoch for each (Exp 5 peaks at 0.546 at epoch 49). Dashed horizontal line at Exp 4 best (0.521) for reference.
**Used in:** Section 4.5 (Experiment 5)

### A5_exp5_deepdive.png
**Title:** Experiment 5: Two-Phase Training with Focal Loss
**Contents:** Two-panel vertically stacked figure. Top panel: Val damage macro F1 over all 60 epochs, coloured by phase (blue = Phase 1 frozen, pink = Phase 2 unfrozen). Dashed vertical line at phase boundary (epoch 10.5). Annotated with best epoch (0.546 at epoch 49). Bottom panel: Per-class F1 (No Damage green, Minor Damage yellow, Major Damage orange, Destroyed red) over all 60 epochs with phase boundary.
**Used in:** Section 4.5 (Experiment 5)
